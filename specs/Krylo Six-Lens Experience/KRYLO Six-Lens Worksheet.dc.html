01 · GOVERNED SEMANTICS

Color is a claim about epistemic status

SPEC I §20.8 requires governed meaning. The three locked hues are assigned to the three positions a band can occupy in the SPEC II revelation flow — never to domain identity, never to severity, never to decoration.

LIME
Corroborated — the condition is supported by evidence that survives inspection.
BLUE
Observed — present and measured, not yet established as meaning it.
PURPLE
Formation-contributing — participates in a structural configuration. Never applied on one observation alone.
ACHROMATIC
Unresolved — colour is withdrawn. The band keeps its surface area, so what cannot be established still occupies the object.

Consequence: a cone that is mostly blue is a cone we are watching. A cone that is mostly lime is a cone we can defend. A cone with a tall grey band is a cone we should not yet speak about.

Radius is inferential distance

SPEC I §10 requires relational placement and evidentiary hierarchy. The rule that satisfies both: how far a halo element sits from the cone is how many inferential steps separate it from the observed object. Nothing is placed for balance.

r₀
Surface. Condition. On the object itself — zero inference.
r₁
Inner halo. Observation and evidence — the provenance the surface rests on.
r₂
Mid halo. Relationship — bearing points toward the related cone's real position.
r₃
Outer halo. Formation — the most interpretive claim, therefore the furthest out.
r∞
Unbound. Unresolved — no anchor line, because nothing yet connects it.

Connector opacity falls with radius. A formation card is legibly the faintest claim on screen without being labelled as such.

SURFACE AREA
IS INFORMATION
Band height is the share of the domain's current state that the condition accounts for — magnitude where the substrate is countable (CAPITAL, LABOR), salience where it is not (MEDIA, KNOWLEDGE). Because the cone tapers, a condition placed near the apex gets less surface for the same weight: apex position is reserved for the acute and the few, base position for the broad and structural. Equal-height banding is a defect, not a fallback (§20.7).

03 · CONVERGENCE CLASSIFIER — PROPOSED MOUNT

KRYL-1207 is built and unmounted. It resolves conflicting narratives without silently choosing one, and it carries a full Data Tap trail. That capability has exactly one honest home in this model.

Mount it at r₁, entering only on RESOLVE, as the element that arbitrates between the evidence card and the unresolved card.

It cannot sit at SEE or INVESTIGATE: a classifier verdict shown before the user has seen the evidence would collapse OBSERVATION into EVIDENCE, which SPEC II §15 forbids implementation from doing. It cannot sit at r₃ with FORMATION: it is the thing that decides whether a formation may be claimed, so it must be nearer the object than the claim it licenses.

Its output has three display states, and the third is not a failure: converged promotes a band to lime; divergent holds the band blue and lists both readings side by side; insufficient withdraws colour from the band and grows the unresolved card. The Data Tap trail is the drill-through from any of the three.

04 · THE SIX LENSES — SPEC I §19 WORKSHEET

Fields in locked order. Cone diagrams show each Lens at INVESTIGATE; band height is weight, band colour is epistemic status per section 01.

LENS 01

CAPITAL

apex → base
velocity · concentration ·
stress · liquidity? ·
reallocation
PURPOSE
The user perceives where capital is currently massing, at what velocity, and under what financing pressure — as one object whose proportions are the domain's state rather than a chart of it.
QUESTION
Where is capital moving, what is changing around that movement, and what does the movement make possible or constrain? (SPEC II §5)
INPUT
Selection of the CAPITAL cone from the environment, or lateral arrival along a financing edge from OWNERSHIP or TECHNOLOGY — in which case the originating cone stays visible on the rail at its true bearing.
TRANSFORMATION
Two coarse bands resolve into five weighted conditions with captions; the other five cones recede to the rail without changing relative order; the halo forms outward from r₁.
OUTPUT
That concentration and strategic reallocation dominate the surface and are corroborated, that mid-cap financing stress is observed but not yet established, and that liquidity pressure is being held open rather than answered.
TRANSITION
Selecting a band moves to RESOLVE for that condition. Selecting a relationship chip carries the user to the related Lens with the capital edge preserved as its input.
STRUCTURAL / PERCEPTUAL
RELATIONSHIP
Flow observation and capital-structure classification become perceptible as proportion. The Data Tap trail behind flow data becomes reachable at RESOLVE rather than being a separate view.
SURFACE ENCODING
Height = share of observed capital movement. Apex = deployment velocity (acute, few actors). Base = strategic reallocation (broad, structural). Markers on the concentration band denote individual vehicles above threshold.
HALO COMPOSITION
r₁ the triggering flow event and, at RESOLVE, its filings; r₂ sources ↔ intermediaries ↔ recipients, bearing toward OWNERSHIP and TECHNOLOGY; r₃ funding-cluster formation, held provisional; unbound, stated purpose versus actual deployment.
LIVE VS. DESIGNED
Live: cone in ConeMap, AnalysisDomainField investigation. Designed: weighted surface bands, captions, all four halo radii, classifier mount, band→RESOLVE transition.

PURPOSE
The user perceives which capability is being taken up, which is being displaced, and how much of the domain's motion is adoption versus infrastructure that has not caught up.
QUESTION
What capability is being introduced, adopted, or displaced, what is changing around it, and what does the change make possible or obsolete? (SPEC II §6)
INPUT
Selection of the TECHNOLOGY cone, or arrival from CAPITAL along a funded-capability edge, from OWNERSHIP along a platform-control edge, or from KNOWLEDGE along a transfer edge.
TRANSFORMATION
The same recomposition, with one addition unique to this Lens: displaced alternatives appear as a negative band — surface removed rather than added — so obsolescence reads as loss of area.
OUTPUT
That adoption momentum is the largest corroborated condition, that infrastructure readiness lags it, and that the gap between claimed and actual capability is large enough to hold a sixth of the surface unresolved.
TRANSITION
Selecting the adoption band opens deployment evidence at RESOLVE. Selecting the displacement apex enters the cascade relationship, which is the one path in this Lens that crosses to LABOR.
STRUCTURAL / PERCEPTUAL
RELATIONSHIP
Adoption and displacement signal detection becomes perceptible as competing surface area. The classifier is load-bearing here: claimed capability is the domain where sources most often diverge.
SURFACE ENCODING
Height = share of observed technical activity. Apex = displacement cascade. Base = capability concentration. Markers denote infrastructure milestones; a hatched edge denotes proprietary opacity where the substrate cannot be observed at all.
HALO COMPOSITION
r₁ the deployment or retirement event, with patent and disclosure records at RESOLVE; r₂ adopters ↔ displaced alternatives ↔ enabling infrastructure; r₃ platform consolidation; unbound, adoption durability.
LIVE VS. DESIGNED
Live: cone, AnalysisDomainField. Designed: negative displacement band, opacity hatching, cascade traversal to LABOR, classifier on the claimed-capability band.

PURPOSE
The user perceives what is holding attention, whether the narrative around it is cohering or fracturing, and how fast it is propagating.
QUESTION
What is receiving attention, how is the narrative forming or fracturing, what is changing around it, and what does the change make more or less possible? (SPEC II §9)
INPUT
Selection of the MEDIA cone, or arrival from any of the other five along an attention edge. MEDIA is the only Lens reachable from all five others, and its halo says which one the user came through.
TRANSFORMATION
Standard recomposition, with one inversion: the r₂ relationship ring points at what the narrative is about, so the cone the user is looking at is partly a lens onto the other five. Those bearings brighten rather than recede.
OUTPUT
That contestation, not consensus, is the dominant observed condition; that propagation velocity is corroborated; and that whether the pattern is coordinated or emergent is unresolved and should not be asserted.
TRANSITION
Selecting the contestation band opens the classifier directly, since this is where divergent readings are the normal case. Selecting a bearing crosses to the domain the narrative concerns.
STRUCTURAL / PERCEPTUAL
RELATIONSHIP
This is the Lens the convergence classifier was effectively built for: conflicting narratives resolved without silently choosing one. Its divergent state is the default display here, not an exception.
SURFACE ENCODING
Height = share of measured attention. Apex = attention monopoly. Base = attention concentration. Markers denote narrative launch and collapse points; band edges are unstable rather than straight where propagation is still moving.
HALO COMPOSITION
r₁ the attention shift, with metrics and timing at RESOLVE; r₂ sources ↔ amplifiers ↔ audiences, and the underlying event the narrative interprets; r₃ propagation pathway or attention monopoly; unbound, actual influence on other domains.
LIVE VS. DESIGNED
Live: cone, AnalysisDomainField. Designed: brightened inverse bearings, unstable band edges, classifier as default display, five-way inbound provenance in the halo.
LENS 06

OWNERSHIP

apex → base
horizontal consolidation ·
control concentration ·
re-bounding · beneficial
control? · repositioning
PURPOSE
The user perceives what has changed hands, how concentrated control has become as a result, and where the chain of control stops being observable.
QUESTION
What changed hands or control, what is changing around that shift, and what does the change make possible or constrain? (SPEC II §10)
INPUT
Selection of the OWNERSHIP cone. In practice this is the most common entry, because control changes originate observations in the other five (SPEC II §11) — so its halo most often shows outbound rather than inbound bearings.
TRANSFORMATION
Standard recomposition. Uniquely, the five other cones do not fully recede: a control change propagates, so the rail keeps the affected cones at partial prominence with their edges drawn.
OUTPUT
That control concentration dominates and is corroborated by filings, that horizontal consolidation is a live formation candidate at the apex, and that ultimate beneficial control remains unestablished for a fifth of the surface.
TRANSITION
Selecting the concentration band opens the filing trail at RESOLVE. Selecting any outbound bearing runs the M&A cross-domain test live: the same event, re-entered from another cut.
STRUCTURAL / PERCEPTUAL
RELATIONSHIP
Entity resolution and beneficial-ownership tracing become perceptible as a chain that visibly terminates. Where the chain ends, the band goes achromatic — opacity is rendered, not omitted.
SURFACE ENCODING
Height = share of controlled assets affected, not transaction count — one large acquisition outranks many small ones. Apex = consolidation formation. Base = strategic repositioning. Markers denote named entities and jurisdictional boundaries crossed.
HALO COMPOSITION
r₁ the control-change announcement, with SEC and beneficial-ownership filings at RESOLVE; r₂ acquirer ↔ target ↔ financing ↔ controlled assets, with a bearing per affected domain; r₃ consolidation formation; unbound, regulatory outcome and ultimate control.
LIVE VS. DESIGNED
Live: cone, AnalysisDomainField. Designed: asset-weighted banding, partial-prominence rail, outbound bearings, terminating-chain encoding, live cross-domain re-entry.
