# LENS CONTRACT (Founder directive, 2026-07-24)

Every lens declares ONE layer. The layer binds its rules of engagement. A lens may not borrow the
other layer's grammar. This is the missing binding between the two existing specs:
- HOW they look → specs/lens-style-standard.md
- HOW the two layers behave → specs/sell-layer-opportunity-architecture.md
- WHICH layer each lens is → this contract.

## THE TWO LAYERS (rules of engagement — NOT the same)

**REPORTING (the Show / virtue / engine)**
- Truth law, absolute: grounded or withhold (§22, "can't calculate, don't show").
- No spin. Shows fractures it doesn't want to show (§20).
- Grammar = a *chart/read*: "here is the field, you interpret." Never asserts a pick.
- May NEVER use sell grammar (no conviction claims, no "the move is X," no aspirational framing).

**SELL (the opportunist / storefront)**
- Persuade, frame, lead, aspire. Bold and brand-shaped is the FUNCTION, not corruption.
- Grammar = a *pitch*: asserts, singular, makes the case for you.
- May aspire, may NEVER wear the reporting costume (no aspiration dressed as a grounded reading).
- MUST keep a drill-down door open to its proof in the reporting layer (sell-to-proof hinge).
- Not audited like the ledger. Reporting-layer rigor does not govern it.

Visibly distinct surfaces. The customer must always know which layer they are reading.

## LENS CLASSIFICATION (LOCKED — Founder 2026-07-24)

| Lens        | Layer      | Grammar                        | Bound rules                    |
|-------------|------------|--------------------------------|--------------------------------|
| SIGNAL      | REPORTING  | heatmap read                   | truth law (§20/§22)            |
| FLOW        | REPORTING  | directional read               | truth law                      |
| PRESSURE    | REPORTING  | constraint read                | truth law                      |
| CONVERGENCE | REPORTING  | gap read (observed vs trend)   | truth law                      |
| DRIFT       | REPORTING  | structural-vs-narrative read   | truth law                      |
| OPPORTUNITY | SELL       | pitch (block + drill-to-proof) | sell rules + sell-to-proof hinge |

Rationale for OPPORTUNITY as the lone SELL lens: it is the only lens whose job is to name an
advantage and make the case for a move. The other five sense and report the field; they do not sell.

## ENFORCEMENT
- A lens's layer is declared and does not change without Founder sign-off.
- REPORTING lenses that begin asserting picks / using conviction language = contract violation.
- The SELL lens that presents a claim with no drill-down to grounded proof = contract violation.
- All lenses, both layers, still conform to lens-style-standard.md for visual consistency (one
  instrument), even though their grammar differs.
