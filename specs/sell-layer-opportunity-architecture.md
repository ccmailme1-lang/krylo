# SELL LAYER — OPPORTUNITY ARCHITECTURE (Founder directive, 2026-07-24)

Founder's architecture. Captured verbatim in intent so it isn't lost. The visual identity is
the Founder's alone (§15) — this spec defines STRUCTURE and RULES OF ENGAGEMENT, not the look.

## 1. THE PLATFORM IS TWO LAYERS WITH DIFFERENT RULES

The platform divides into two roles, and they do NOT answer to the same law:

- **THE SHOW (virtue / reporting layer)** — the engine. Grounded or withhold. No spin. Shows
  fractures it doesn't want to show (§20). Never fabricates (§22, "can't calculate, don't show").
  Truth law is absolute here. This is the incorruptible core (Engine vs. Brand doctrine).

- **THE SELL (opportunist / sell layer)** — OPPORTUNITY. Its job is to make the case: persuade,
  frame, lead, aspire. It is ALLOWED to be bold and brand-shaped — that is its function, not a
  corruption. Holding the sell layer to withhold-and-ground is strangling the storefront.

## 2. THE ONE LINE BETWEEN THEM (load-bearing)

The sell layer may **aspire**, but it may **never wear the reporting layer's costume.**
- Aspiration presented AS aspiration = honest selling.
- Aspiration dressed as a grounded reading = fabrication — it has borrowed the Show's authority.
- Therefore the two must be **visibly distinct surfaces.** The customer must always know which
  one they are reading. The sell never poses as the engine.

Reporting-layer rigor (can't-calculate-can't-show) governs the SHOW. It does NOT govern the SELL.
Do not audit the storefront like the ledger.

## 3. HOW THE SELL LAYER RENDERS OPPORTUNITY

Not a chart — a chart is reporting grammar ("here's the data, you interpret"). The sell layer's
grammar is a **pitch**: it makes the case for you.

- **It asserts** — names the move, paints the upside, takes a stance. Conviction is the job.
- **It's singular** — one opportunity foregrounded. A portfolio of six is a report; a pick is a pitch.
- **It carries its own proof one layer down** — the honest hinge (see §4).

## 6a. THE SELL LAYER IS "THE SCOUTING REPORT" (name + form, Founder 2026-07-24)

The sell-layer opportunity surface is **The Scouting Report** — not "the call."
- A *call* tells the reader what to do (advice → drifts toward prediction).
- A *scouting report* reports what the field is doing — observed, forming moves — and lets the
  reader draw their own play. That is DETECTION, not prediction: forward-leaning but grounded.
  ("See competitors' moves while they're still forming" — observation of early signal, not forecast.)

FORM (agent-generated report modal — ref CB Insights Scouting Report):
1. Header — "Scouting Report" + Export / Copy link (a portable document, not a dashboard).
2. Provenance line — "Generated by [Scouting] Agent on [timestamp]" (Observation Law: timestamped,
   attributed, sourced).
3. Vitals block — the domain's macro key-values.
4. Score row — 2–3 composite scores side by side, each with a one-line gloss (the salient hook —
   Krylo's leverage / edge / convergence scores, the Mosaic-equivalent).
5. Prose summary — the macro read in plain language.
6. Key Takeaways — bulleted, forward-leaning, **each carrying a citation** — the citation IS the
   sell-to-proof hinge, native to the format.
7. Expandable detail rows — the individual scouted moves behind the summary (the drill-down list).

Register: macro, salient, no deep dive (the deep dive is the drill-down destination). It is the
domains' prospectus / hype reel — persuasive on top, engine underneath.

## 6b. IT CLOSES THE LOOP — ROLLUP OF THE REPORTING LENSES (Founder 2026-07-24)

The Scouting Report is the sell-layer **rollup of the entire reporting layer.** The six lenses were
never peers — FIVE SENSE, ONE SYNTHESIZES:
- SIGNAL → how hot the domain is
- FLOW → which way it's moving
- PRESSURE → what's constraining it
- CONVERGENCE → how strongly the signals agree
- DRIFT → structure pulling away from narrative (the early tell)
→ synthesized into a composite leverage score + prose read + Key Takeaways.

CITATIONS RESOLVE TO SOURCE LENSES: each takeaway footnotes the lens that sensed it
("structure is pulling ahead of the narrative `[DRIFT]`"). Drilling the citation lands in that
lens's grounded reading — the reporting lenses ARE the receipts behind the report's claims.

This is the loop closing (§19): the sell-layer emission (the report) is tied to its grounded source
(the lenses), auditable end to end. The storefront is literally assembled from the ledger, every
persuasive line a cited rollup of a grounded read. Honest by construction.
