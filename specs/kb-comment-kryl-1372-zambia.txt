Acceptance for this fixture: subject text = The Republic of Zambia; objective = the deal-evaluation value; attribution UNRESOLVED if no registry entity resolves; field-level output stays labeled field-level and is not attributed to the subject; the contract is the one object every downstream surface (brief, left column, MAP, domain tabs) reads.
