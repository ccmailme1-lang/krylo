# Where the product stands, what decisions it needs, and what it takes to reach real-world value

Date 2026-10-11 · Prepared for the Founder · Facts are from the milestone-1 report, the Oracle / Broadcom / Ford pages of 2026-10-11 and the recorded reviews. Judgments are marked as such.

## 1. Are we close to real-world value? (judgment)

- **What exists and is real:** for a listed US company, an evidence-labelled picture of who discloses ownership, what changed over two 12-month periods, and which capital events (offerings, underwriting and distribution agreements) the company reported and with which confirmed counterparties. Every statement traces to a filing accession. Oracle shows this well (10 admitted relationships, a CAPITAL to OWNERSHIP formation).
- **What does not exist:** relationships in TECHNOLOGY, KNOWLEDGE, LABOR and MEDIA. Broadcom and Ford therefore read as ownership-only analyses. Bank counterparties such as Deutsche Bank, J.P. Morgan and BNP Paribas are still unmatched.
- **Scores:** mechanical 7.943 (30 of 35 cases at 8 or above); my rating of the Broadcom page about 7; GPT's independent rating of the same page 5.5; "Goldman approved" is 10. None of these is a blind expert review.
- **Honest position:** close to a credible, narrow product (SEC ownership and financing evidence with provenance, for public companies). Not close to the six-domain structural intelligence the vision describes, and not yet at the level a senior banker would sign off.

## 2. The questions to ask (decisions that are the Founder's)

1. **Who is the first user, and what question do they bring on Monday?** Pick one job (for example "who owns this company, who financed it, and what changed"), and collect 10 real questions from 2 to 3 real people in that role. These replace the invented benchmark questions as the test.
2. **Is a narrow, excellent product the first release, or must all six domains be present?** The evidence supports ownership plus capital first; the other four wait on data sources and ratified types.
3. **Which relationship types, from which sources, for each remaining domain?** Needs a proposal per domain naming the source, the identifier that ties it to a company, the admission rule and the evidence a reader sees. Candidate sources that already exist in the code: patent assignments (TECHNOLOGY). For LABOR, KNOWLEDGE and MEDIA no source is wired.
4. **What must a page look like?** One lead finding first, then evidence, with definitions and diagnostics moved out of the main read. That is a presentation decision for the Founder.
5. **Who judges "approved"?** Two or three finance professionals scoring blind on the frozen rubric, kept separate from my scoring.
6. **Is inference allowed in the headline?** Today inferred statements are labelled and never headline. Say if a reader-useful "so what" line is wanted and under what labels.
