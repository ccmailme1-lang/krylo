# KRYLO — Go-To-Market Strategy

**Status:** DRAFT (2026-10-07). Subordinate to `specs/PROBLEM-STATEMENT-and-commercial-funnel.md`
(FROZEN). Numbers marked **HYPOTHESIS** have no precedent in this repo and need a Founder decision
or evidence before use.

---

## 1. The problem we sell against

> "I have plenty of information. I don't know which separate signals are becoming meaningfully
> related."

We do not sell more data or better predictions. We sell visibility into **what is forming** across
information the buyer already has too much of.

## 2. Buyers (from the frozen spec §5)

Target people whose **job is to detect change**. They already believe cross-domain change matters;
their problem is seeing the relationships.

| Buyer | They wake up asking | What KRYLO presents |
|---|---|---|
| Investment research | What structural change is emerging beneath the market narrative? | Cross-domain relationships relevant to a thesis |
| Corporate strategy | What external change is beginning to alter our operating environment? | Emerging structural shifts before they are conventional wisdom |
| Government / resilience | Where are multiple systems under the same structural pressure? | Convergence across otherwise separate systems |
| Demographic strategy | What is population change beginning to restructure? | Demographic pressure across labor, capital, technology, ownership, knowledge |
| Technology / AI strategy | Where is adoption beginning to reorganize other systems? | Second- and third-order structural effects |

Industries (semiconductors, biopharma, automotive, logistics, defense, media) are **subjects**
these buyers point KRYLO at — see `docs/market/problem-markets.md`.

**Deprioritize:** buyers who want a fixed-metric dashboard or a recommendation engine. KRYLO does
not tell anyone what to do; a buyer who needs that will be disappointed.

## 3. Entry wedge

**Single-subject structural read.** The buyer names one live subject (a company, a deal, a
disruption). KRYLO presents the relationships and formation it observes, with the evidence behind
each. The buyer judges whether it changed their understanding.

- Pilot length: **HYPOTHESIS** 14–30 days.
- Pilot fee: **HYPOTHESIS** — no precedent found. Founder decision required.
- Pass criterion: the buyer identifies at least one relationship they had not assembled themselves
  and can trace it to its evidence.

## 4. Funnel (frozen spec §6)

```
ENTRY        "Something changed."
ORIENTATION  "Show me what is moving."
DISCOVERY    "Show me what is connected."
VALIDATION   "Is this relationship supported by evidence?"
FORMATION    "Is something structurally forming?"
INSPECTION   "What evidence created this formation?"
```

Lead with the buyer's professional question, demonstrate, then let them look. Do not lead with
"Structural Intelligence Platform" or a choose-a-domain step.

## 5. Messaging

**Opener.** "You already have terminals, research, and analysts. When something shifts three
suppliers down, or across two domains, who connects it — and how long does that take?"

**Contrast.** "Your tools tell you a factory is delayed. They don't show you which other
observations that delay is now connected to. KRYLO shows the relationship and the evidence behind
it."

**Close.** "Point it at something you're working on now. You decide what it means."

**Never say:** predicts, forecasts, recommends, "before the market," "the only platform," or any
claim about a real event KRYLO has not been tested against (`docs/product/positioning.md` §6).

## 6. 90-day sequence (HYPOTHESIS — no prior campaign in repo)

| Days | Action | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1–30 | Build a named target list per buyer type; run the retrospective tests in `problem-markets.md` | Target list; first evidence-log entries |
| 31–60 | Outreach using a *real, tested* retrospective as the artifact — not a hypothetical | Discovery calls |
| 61–90 | Convert to single-subject reads (§3) | Pilots with pass/fail recorded |

Outreach does not start with a hypothetical case study. It starts once at least one retrospective
result exists.

## 7. Scorecard

Definitions live in `docs/data-room/metrics.md`. Targets are **HYPOTHESIS** until a baseline
exists.

| Metric | Measures |
|---|---|
| Discovery → pilot conversion | Whether the problem lands |
| Time to first admitted relationship on a guest's subject | Time to value |
| Pilot pass rate (§3 criterion) | Whether what KRYLO presents is new to the buyer |
| Pilot → paid conversion | Willingness to pay |
| Returning sessions per guest | Activity — not impact (CLAUDE.md §20.6) |

## 8. Known risk from the guest pilot

Guest "J" (2026-09-22 report): "all of the questions I asked came back with no real response." The
cause traced to the designed no-live-signal path. To a guest, an honest absence and a broken
product look the same. Onboarding and absence states must explain *why* nothing is shown
(`specs/REPORT-guest-pilot-verified-findings-20260922.md` §2.2, Mechanism B).
