Your first session with KRYLO

KRYLO watches six domains — capital, ownership, technology, knowledge, labor, and media — for structural change and relationships forming across them. It detects emerging structure; it does not predict what happens next.

Bring KRYLO a question from your world. Explore what it surfaces, follow the relationships that interest you, and see where the structure takes you.

Getting in

Sign in and orient

  1. Open krylo.org. When the access panel appears, enter the access code you were given.
  2. You'll arrive at Home — the Macro-level Domain Field Observatory.
  3. When you're ready, open Analysis and enter a real situation or question from your world — not a list of keywords.
  4. Submit. KRYLO will build a Target Packet in Analysis for you to explore.

What to run

Five queries, in this order

These five queries are designed to give you different ways to experience KRYLO. Run them in order, as naturally as you can.

  1. 01 · Sparse

    "Looking to buy a commercial unit in New York City."

    The situation is intentionally underspecified. Run it as you would if this were real.

  2. 02 · Same subject, fully formed

    Return to the same purchase and add: the decision on the table — buy, lease, or redevelop — a budget, and a timeline.

    Compare what surfaces against query 01.

  3. 03 · A named situation in your field

    Bring something concrete you'd actually want to understand — an entity building a position, a technology being adopted, a workforce shift, or another situation from your field.

    Before you run it, note what you expect to find. Then compare what you expected with what KRYLO surfaces.

  4. 04 · Deliberately vague

    "What should I do about my situation?"

    Run it as written.

  5. 05 · Capital or ownership formation

    Bring a question about companies, stakes, debt, or capital reorganizing in a sector you know.

    Run it as you naturally would.

Reading the screen

What each surface is

KRYLO is organized around several views:

Home
The Macro-level Domain Field Observatory.
Analysis
Explore the question you brought.
Formation
Examine the structural relationships KRYLO surfaces.
News Feed
Follow relevant developments in the field.
Community
See perspectives and activity from others.
History
Revisit previous questions and sessions.

Move between views as you explore. There is no required order.

A note on what you may see. Some fields may say "NOT MEASURED" or "no verified record." If you encounter either, form your own view of what you are seeing and whether you trust it. You'll be asked about this afterward.

After your session

You'll complete a short questionnaire

As you go, note anything that stands out — anything that feels unclear, unsupported, contradictory, unexpected, or that changes how you see the situation.

Immediately after the session, you'll be asked about what you noticed, on your own terms: open the questionnaire.
